## Verdict and why
**NOT THIS WAY.** The goal (winter income, keeping the full-time crew busy) is right. Commercial plowing is the wrong route: it means new equipment, 24-hour on-call work, a kind of liability Copperleaf has never carried, and customers outside the business's model. A narrower version could work.

## Concept
Contracts to plow and salt commercial parking lots through the winter, starting with the strip mall that asked.

## What it builds on
- Two trucks and a full-time crew that are underused in winter.
- A request from a real potential customer.

## The value
1. Winter revenue.
2. Year-round work for the full-time crew.

## Honest risks and tradeoffs
- **Liability:** slip-and-fall claims on commercial lots are a major exposure, and the current policy doesn't cover snow.
- **Equipment:** plows, a salt spreader and possibly a loader. Likely over the $15,000 risk budget.
- **On-call hours:** storms at 3 a.m. That's the opposite of Rosa's goal of fewer hours.
- **Off-model:** the brief says "will not do commercial property maintenance."
- **"A customer asked":** Rosa's named pattern of saying yes too fast.

## Panel notes
- **Finance (caution):** equipment plus insurance could cost more than one season's contract revenue, and income swings with snowfall.
- **Legal-risk (oppose):** commercial snow liability is the biggest exposure on the list. Would need new coverage, written contracts and documented service logs.
- **Operations-tech (oppose):** on-call nights with five people means burnout before February.
- **Customer-voice (caution):** existing customers wouldn't notice, unless the crew is exhausted in spring.

## The version that could work
**Residential snow clearing for prepaid care plan customers only:** driveways and walks for existing clients, scheduled after storms (not on-call for businesses), using a walk-behind blower and the existing trucks. It adds winter value to the care plan and stays inside the customer base Copperleaf knows.

## Smallest test
After the care plan test, survey plan customers: "Would you add winter driveway clearing for $X a month?" Price it only after the insurance agent confirms coverage and cost.

## What would change the verdict
Insurance quotes that make residential snow affordable and at least 15 plan customers saying yes.
