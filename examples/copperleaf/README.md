# Example: Copperleaf Landscape Co.

> **Fictional.** Copperleaf Landscape Co., its owner, staff, town and numbers are invented for this demo. Any resemblance to a real business is a coincidence.

This is what the kit produces once the starter agent has interviewed an owner. Use it to see the finished shape before you build your own. **Don't copy it into your business**: an advisor built from someone else's business gives someone else's advice.

## The business in one line

A nine-year-old landscape design and maintenance company in the fictional town of Alder Falls, run by owner Rosa Delacroix with a crew of five full-time and two seasonal staff.
