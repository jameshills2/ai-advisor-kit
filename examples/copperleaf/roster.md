# Copperleaf's Roster (FICTIONAL EXAMPLE)

What the starter agent recommended after Rosa's interview, and what she accepted.

## Recommended and accepted

| Role | Why Copperleaf needs it |
|---|---|
| **Juniper (lead advisor / strategist)** | Always included. Rosa's goal is fewer hours and steadier cash, so every idea gets weighed against both. |
| **Finance** | Winter cash flow is the stated 12-month goal, and margins by service line are UNKNOWN. Most ideas will live or die on cash timing. |
| **Legal-risk** | Copperleaf works on customer property, employs seasonal staff and uses one-page estimates with no formal terms. Liability questions come up on most new offers. |

| **Customer-voice** | 70% of work is referrals. Anything that disappoints existing customers puts the growth engine at risk. |
