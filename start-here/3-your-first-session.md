# 3. Your First Session

Plan on 30 to 45 minutes without interruptions.

## Before you start, gather

- Revenue by source for the last 12 months (rough numbers are fine)
- Three ideas you tried that didn't work, and why you think they failed
- Your hard limits: hours per week, money you can risk, people you can call on, anything you won't do
- Where your parked ideas will live (a folder, Notion or Google Drive)

## The steps

1. **Open a new chat** in the tool you picked (not inside an existing project yet).
2. **Copy the whole starter agent** from [`kit/starter-agent.md`](../kit/starter-agent.md), everything inside the big text box, and paste it in.
3. **Answer the interview.** It asks one or two questions at a time. Be specific and honest. "I don't know" is a fine answer; it gets recorded so you can fill it in later.
4. **Review your roster.** The starter agent recommends which advisor roles fit your business (for example: a strategist, a finance lens and a legal-risk lens). Add, remove or ask for a role it didn't suggest.
5. **Collect your documents.** It writes your business brief, your lead advisor, your roles and your idea template.
6. **Set it up** using the guide for your tool in [`kit/platform-guides/`](../kit/platform-guides/). Usually that means creating a project, pasting the advisor into the instructions and uploading your brief.
7. **Test it with a real idea.** Something you've been chewing on. See how it pushes back.

## Your first idea review

Start with this question. It works on any idea:

> Before you agree with me, tell me what has to be true for this to work, and which of those things I haven't shown you yet.

Then ask for a **panel review** if you want the specialist viewpoints, and say **"park this"** to save the write-up.

## When it gets something wrong

It will. It only knows what you've told it. Tell it what it missed and why. The correction makes the advice better, and adding it to your brief makes the next session better too.

## Keep it fresh

Update your business brief every quarter, or whenever something big changes. An advisor working from old goals gives old advice.
