# 2. Pick Your Tool

Use the AI tool you already pay for. You don't need to switch.

| If you use... | Use this feature | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Claude** (claude.ai) | Projects | Advisor instructions + your brief stay loaded in every chat in the project. |
| **ChatGPT** | Projects, or a Custom GPT | Same idea. A Custom GPT gives your advisor its own name and icon. |
| **Gemini** | Gems | Same idea, and write-ups save easily to Google Docs. |
| **Claude Code** (or another agent tool) | Agents in your project folder | The full version: separate role agents, ideas researched in the background and saved as files automatically. |

**Not sure?** Start with whichever chat tool you already use. You can move to the agent version later; the files are the same.

## A few honest notes

- **Use a paid plan if you can.** Free tiers often limit project features, file uploads or how much the AI can read at once, and your brief plus your idea library will grow.
- **The chat version needs a place to save ideas.** A folder of documents, a Notion database or a Google Drive folder all work. See [`kit/idea-inbox/`](../kit/idea-inbox/).
- **Your business information goes into the tool you choose.** Check that tool's data and privacy settings before you paste in revenue numbers or anything confidential, and leave out anything you wouldn't want stored.
