# The Kit

Everything you need to build your own advisor system. New to this? Start at [`../start-here/`](../start-here/) instead.

## Order of operations

1. **[`starter-agent.md`](starter-agent.md)** - run it first. It interviews you, recommends your roster and generates your documents from the templates below.
2. **[`advisor/`](advisor/)** - the lead advisor template, if you want to edit by hand.
3. **[`roles/`](roles/)** - the role template, the role builder and the library. Your roster is yours: keep, edit, delete or add.
4. **[`idea-inbox/`](idea-inbox/)** - pick where ideas live (folder, Notion or Google Drive) and set up the idea writer.
5. **[`platform-guides/`](platform-guides/)** - put it all into your tool.
6. **[`corpus-prompts/`](corpus-prompts/)** - use it: panel reviews, connecting ideas, mining articles, revisiting "not now."

## The role library

| Role | Lens | Typical fit |
|---|---|---|
| [strategist](roles/library/strategist.md) | Goals, focus, timing | Everyone (usually your lead advisor) |
| [finance](roles/library/finance.md) | Cash, cost, pricing, payback | Anyone spending money on ideas |
| [legal-risk](roles/library/legal-risk.md) | Contracts, liability, regulation | Service businesses, contracts, regulated work |
| [marketing](roles/library/marketing.md) | Demand, message, channels | New offers and launches |
| [operations-tech](roles/library/operations-tech.md) | Delivery, people, tools | Anything that adds work or systems |
| [security](roles/library/security.md) | Data, access, automation risk | Online businesses, AI automation, customer data |
| [customer-voice](roles/library/customer-voice.md) | Your best customer's reaction | Everyone |
| [design-ux](roles/library/design-ux.md) | Ease, clarity, trust | Websites, apps, booking and buying flows |

Need something else, like a franchise expert, HR, a pricing specialist or a sustainability lens? Use the [role builder](roles/role-builder.md).
