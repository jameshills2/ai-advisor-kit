# Role Builder

Need a viewpoint the library doesn't have? A lawyer, a pricing specialist, a franchise expert, a "skeptical customer," an HR lens, a sustainability lens? Paste the prompt below into your advisor chat (or any chat with your business brief loaded) and fill in the first line.

```text
I want to add a role to my advisor panel: [describe it, e.g. "a lawyer who looks at
contracts and liability" or "a pricing specialist"].

Using my business brief, write the role definition with:
- Name and one-line description
- Its lens (one sentence)
- The 5 questions it always asks, specific to MY business
- What it deliberately ignores (what other roles cover)
- 3 to 5 red flags it always calls out, specific to my business
- How it reports: 3 to 6 bullets, the biggest risk or opportunity, what evidence would
  change its view, and a lean of support / caution / oppose
- Limits: if this role overlaps a licensed profession (law, accounting, tax, medicine,
  engineering, financial advice), it must say it spots issues and prepares questions for a
  qualified professional and does not replace one

Before writing it, ask me up to 3 questions if you need to understand what I want this role
to catch. Then check: does this role overlap one I already have? If so, tell me and suggest
whether to merge them.
```

## Tips

- **Keep roles narrow.** A role that looks at everything is just a second advisor.
- **Three to five roles is plenty.** More roles means longer reviews, not better ones.
- **Retire roles you don't use.** If a role hasn't changed your mind in months, delete it.
- **Name the blind spot.** The most useful roles cover what you personally tend to skip. If you're a big-picture person, add an operations role. If you're a perfectionist, add a "ship it" role.
