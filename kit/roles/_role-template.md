# Role Template

A role is one viewpoint on your panel. It sees every idea through a single lens and deliberately ignores the rest, so you get several sharp views instead of one blurry one.

Copy this file, rename it (e.g. `pricing.md`), and fill in the brackets. Or use [`role-builder.md`](role-builder.md) and have your AI write it for you.

```yaml
---
name: [role-name]
description: [One line - what this role looks at and when to call it.]
---
```
