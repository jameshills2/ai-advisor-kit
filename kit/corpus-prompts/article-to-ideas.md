# Article To Ideas

You read a trade publication, a competitor announcement or a news story and something clicks. This prompt turns it into opportunities by checking it against your business and your parked ideas.

```text
Here's an article: [paste the text or link]

1. In 3 bullets: what in this matters for MY business, based on my brief? If nothing
   does, say so and stop.
2. Check it against my parked ideas. Which ideas does it:
   - strengthen (new evidence, a new tool, a shift in demand)?
   - weaken (a competitor got there first, a cost went up, a rule changed)?
   - connect (two parked ideas that now fit together)?
3. Is there a new idea here worth parking? If so, give it a one-paragraph summary and a
   first-read verdict.
4. Be skeptical of the article itself: what's the source's angle, and what would you want
   to verify before acting on it?
```
