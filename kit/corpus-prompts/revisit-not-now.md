# Revisit A "Not Now"

A NOT NOW is a bet that something will change. This prompt checks whether it has.

Use it whenever you browse your library, when a revisit date comes due, or when the business changes (new hire, new tool, a good or bad quarter).

```text
Revisit this parked idea: [idea file or title]

1. Read its "What would change the verdict" line. Has that happened? What's the evidence?
2. What else has changed in my business brief since it was parked?
3. Are any newer parked ideas related to it?
4. Give an updated verdict (it can stay NOT NOW) and why.
5. If the verdict changed, update the write-up: new verdict, today's date, what changed,
   and a new revisit signal. Keep the old verdict in a short history line.
```

## Browsing, not clearing

You don't have to revisit everything on a schedule. The library works best as something you wander through when you have a few minutes: read an old idea, notice a connection, ask the advisor about it. The ideas don't expire.
