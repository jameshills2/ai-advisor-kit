# Connect A New Idea To Your Library

New ideas often aren't new. They're a better version of something you parked, a missing piece for an old idea, or a repeat of something that already failed. This prompt finds out which.

Give the advisor access to your idea library first (the folder in Claude Code; uploaded files, an export or a connector in chat tools).

```text
Here's a new idea: [idea]

Before reviewing it, check it against my parked ideas:
1. Is this a repeat or a close variant of something already parked? Which one, and what
   verdict did it get? What's different this time?
2. Does it supply a missing piece for any NOT NOW or NOT THIS WAY idea? Quote that idea's
   "What would change the verdict" line.
3. Does it conflict with or compete for the same time and money as any BUILD IT idea?
4. Do any NOT A GOOD IDEA lessons apply here?

Then give me your normal review and verdict, and list the related ideas so the write-up
can link them.
```
