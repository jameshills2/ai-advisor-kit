# Setup: ChatGPT

Two options. Projects are quicker; a Custom GPT gives your advisor its own name, icon and conversation starters.

## Option A: a Project

1. **Run the starter agent** in a regular new chat and collect your documents.
2. **Create a Project** named after your advisor.
3. **Project instructions:** paste your lead advisor, then your roles under "Panel roles," then the idea template.
4. **Project files:** upload your business brief, and optionally your parked ideas.
5. Start idea conversations inside the project. Say "park this" to get a write-up, then save it to your Idea Inbox.

## Option B: a Custom GPT

1. Create a GPT. In **Configure**:
   - **Name:** your advisor's name
   - **Instructions:** lead advisor + roles + idea template (shorten if you hit the length limit; roles can move to a knowledge file)
   - **Knowledge:** business brief, roles file if moved, optionally parked ideas
   - **Conversation starters:** "Here's an idea...", "Run a panel review on...", "Check this article against my ideas", "Revisit a NOT NOW idea"
2. Keep it **private** (only you), since it contains your business details.

**Tips**
- Turn off settings that use your conversations for training if your business information is sensitive; check your plan's data controls.
- Update the brief file whenever your business changes.
