# Setup: Gemini Gems

1. **Run the starter agent** in a regular Gemini chat and collect your documents.
2. **Create a Gem** named after your advisor.
3. **Instructions:** paste your lead advisor, then your roles under "Panel roles," then the idea template.
4. **Knowledge:** attach your business brief (a Google Doc works well, since you can keep it updated in one place).
5. **Idea Inbox:** use the Google Drive setup in [`../idea-inbox/setup-google-drive.md`](../idea-inbox/setup-google-drive.md). Save each write-up as a Doc in your Idea Inbox folder.
6. Start idea conversations with your Gem. Say "park this" to get the write-up.

**Tips**
- Attach the Idea Index sheet when you want the advisor to look for connections between ideas.
- Keep the Gem private; it contains your business details.
