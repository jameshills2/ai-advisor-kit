# Setup: Claude Projects (claude.ai)

1. **Run the starter agent** in a regular new chat and collect your documents (brief, advisor, roles, idea template).
2. **Create a Project** named after your advisor, e.g. "Sage - Business Advisor."
3. **Project instructions:** paste your lead advisor definition. Underneath it, paste your role definitions under a heading "Panel roles," followed by the idea template under "Idea template."
4. **Project knowledge:** upload your business brief. Optionally upload your parked ideas (or an export of them) so the advisor can connect new ideas to old ones.
5. **Start every idea conversation inside the project.** Try: "Here's an idea... Before you agree with me, tell me what has to be true."
6. **Park ideas:** say "park this." Save the write-up to your Idea Inbox (folder, Notion or Drive), and add it to the project knowledge if you want the advisor to see it next time.

**Tips**
- If a connector for Notion or Google Drive is enabled, the advisor can save write-ups there directly. Check what it saved.
- When your brief changes, replace the file in project knowledge rather than adding a second version, so the advisor never reads stale goals.
