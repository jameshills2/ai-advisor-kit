# Idea Template

One idea per document. Keep every one, including the rejected ideas: your library is a record of your thinking, not a to-do list.

Copy everything below the line.

---

```markdown
---
title: "Short, specific title"
date: YYYY-MM-DD
source: "What sparked it - a conversation, an article, a customer request"
verdict: BUILD IT | NOT THIS WAY | NOT NOW | NOT A GOOD IDEA
revisit: "The date or signal to look at it again"
related: [other-idea-file-names]
---

## Verdict and why
One or two sentences. The reason matters more than the label.

## Concept
What it is, in plain words. How it would work.

## What it builds on
What the business already has that makes this easier: customers, skills, tools, reputation, past work. If the answer is "nothing", say so.

## The value
1. Concrete win
2. Concrete win
3. Concrete win

## Honest risks and tradeoffs
What could go wrong, what it costs, what it pulls attention from, and how each risk could be reduced.

## Panel notes
One line per role that reviewed it, with its lean (support / caution / oppose).

## Smallest test
The cheapest, fastest way to prove or kill it.

## What would change the verdict
The specific signal, number or event that would make this worth reconsidering.
```

---

## The four verdicts

| Verdict | Means | The write-up must include |
|---|---|---|
| **BUILD IT** | The case holds up | The first small step |
| **NOT THIS WAY** | Good idea, wrong approach | The version that could work |
| **NOT NOW** | Good idea, wrong time or missing pieces | What has to change first |
| **NOT A GOOD IDEA** | The case doesn't hold up | The lesson, so you spot it next time |
