# Contributing

Thanks for helping the kit grow. The most useful contributions are **new roles** for the library, and fixes that make the kit easier for people who have never done this before.

## Contribute a role

1. Copy [`kit/roles/_role-template.md`](kit/roles/_role-template.md) to `kit/roles/library/your-role-name.md` (lowercase, hyphens).
2. Fill in every section: lens, the 5 questions, what it ignores, red flags, how it reports, and limits.
3. Keep it **general**. A library role should work for many businesses. The starter agent and role builder customize it for each owner.
4. Add a credit line at the bottom:
   ```markdown
   ---
   Contributed by [Your Name](https://your-site.com)
   ```
5. Add a row for your role to the table in [`kit/README.md`](kit/README.md).
6. Open a pull request saying what kind of business the role helps and one example of something it would catch.

### What gets accepted

- **Narrow lenses.** A role that reviews everything is a second advisor, not a role.
- **Real red flags,** the kind someone with experience in that field would call out.
- **Honest limits.** Any role touching a licensed profession (law, accounting, tax, medicine, engineering, financial advice) must say it spots issues and prepares questions for a qualified professional, and does not replace one.
- **No promotion.** Your credit line is welcome. Product pitches, affiliate links and tracking inside the role text are not.
- **The house style:** plain language, no hype, and no em or en dashes (use " - ").

## Fixes and improvements

Typos, broken links, clearer steps and platform guide updates (AI tools change their menus often) are always welcome. Open a pull request, or an issue if you'd rather just point it out.

## Share what you built
